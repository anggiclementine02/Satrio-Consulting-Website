03

Employer of Record
(EOR)

Hire talent across Asia without setting up a legal entity. As your Employer of Record, Satrio Consulting takes full legal responsibility for employment, payroll processing, tax compliance, and benefits administration — so you can focus on growth.

Our EOR service allows you to onboard talent in days, not months, across Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, and beyond.

  • Legal employment in 10+ Asian markets
  • Full payroll management & tax compliance
  • Local benefits administration
  • Employment contracts & HR documentation
  • Work permit & visa facilitation
  • Offboarding & termination support

OKOSHI Japanese

From N5 beginner to business-level N2 proficiency — we take you the full distance.

  • Business communication and etiquette training

  • Visa and relocation assistance
  • Alumni mentorship network in Japan
