Our Story

Built from a Belief in Human Potential

Satrio Consulting was founded in 2016 with a simple but powerful belief: the right talent transforms organisations. What began as a boutique search firm in Jakarta has grown into a regional talent powerhouse serving clients across Southeast Asia and beyond.

Our founders, seasoned professionals from the worlds of HR, finance, and operations, recognised a critical gap — companies were struggling to access quality talent in emerging markets, while skilled professionals were struggling to find roles worthy of their ambitions.

We built Satrio Consulting to close that gap. Today, we operate at the intersection of precision search, technology, and a deeply human understanding of what makes careers and companies thrive.
